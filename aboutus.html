// MATRIX CORE //

In the heart of the system, behind every flicker of code and surge of innovation, operates the Core Committee of Tech Trek 3.0 — a collective not just of organizers, but of architects of experience. Moving like unseen processes within a matrix, we synchronize design, technology, and execution to construct something far beyond a conventional event. Every pixel, every interaction, every challenge is engineered with intent. We are thinkers, builders, and disruptors — students who refuse to settle for the surface layer. From orchestrating high-stakes competitions to crafting immersive digital environments, we function as a unified system, each node critical, each decision calculated. This isn’t just an event.

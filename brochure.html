INNOVATE. INSPIRE. IGNITE.

LOG_ENTRY // 001

INVITATION

Greetings from Delhi Public School Vadodara!

We are delighted to announce Tech Trek 3.0, the third edition of our flagship Inter-School IT Fest, to be held on Saturday, 22 August 2026.

This much-awaited event promises to be an inspiring celebration of technology, innovation, and creativity, bringing together some of the brightest young minds from across schools. Tech Trek 3.0 provides an exciting platform where imagination meets technology, empowering students to transform their ideas into innovative solutions.

In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, we invite your talented students to showcase their creativity, technical expertise, and innovative spirit through three exciting competitions. From designing visually compelling thumbnails and engineering robots to race against challenging tracks, to demonstrating knowledge and quick thinking in an engaging IT Quiz — every event has been thoughtfully curated to inspire creativity, collaboration, critical thinking, and excellence.

We are confident that your school's enthusiastic participation will make Tech Trek 3.0 a truly memorable celebration of talent, innovation, and the spirit of healthy competition. We look forward to welcoming your students and receiving your esteemed participation.

Warm Regards,
Team — Tech Trek 3.0
Delhi Public School Vadodara

GENERAL GUIDELINES

  • DATE OF EVENT: 22 August 2026 (Saturday)
  • REPORTING TIME: 08:00 A.M.
  • Each team will be allowed to participate in any one event only.
  • Maximum 2 teams from each school can participate in all the 3 events.
  • Participants must abide by the eligibility criteria set for each event.
  • The decision of the judges will be final.

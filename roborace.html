RULES

  • MAXIMUM: 2 members per team.
  • REPAIRS: Once a robot has passed inspection, no repairs or part replacements are permitted between heats.
  • SPORTSMANSHIP: Any attempt to tamper with another team's robot will result in disqualification.
  • SIGNAL INTERFERENCE: Deliberate interference with another team's RF or Bluetooth control is strictly prohibited.
  • MANUAL INTERVENTION: Teams may manually assist their robot up to two times during a run — each intervention carries a 15-point penalty.
  • TIME LIMIT: To be announced on the day of the event.
